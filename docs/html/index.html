The idea in one paragraph

A statement is who said it, plus subject → predicate → object, plus what backs it. People record statements. Statements that say the same thing are grouped automatically into a shared proposition. Evidence for and against a proposition is organized into evidence lines, and a synthesis assessment is itself a statement, made by EcoWeaver, whose evidence is other people's statements.

Why "WARP"? On a loom, the warp is the set of threads held fixed under tension; everything else is woven across it. WARP is that fixed structure for EcoWeaver: statements, propositions, evidence and scope stay the same, while node templates and predicate groups are the weft that changes with each kind of statement.
