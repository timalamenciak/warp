Advanced user guide

How WARP works

The full conceptual model, for people who want to know why a suggestion is recorded the way it is, read WARP data, or write suggestions in WARP's own format. Building and maintaining the database is covered in the mechanic guide.

The overall shape

WARP is a profile of the SEPIO Core Information Model, a general model for scientific evidence and provenance. It reuses the Causal Mosaic (CAMO) for everything specific to causal claims. WARP adds the ecology: scope, practitioner and Indigenous knowledge, ecological evidence types, and node templates.

PartWhat it isComes from
Node shapesFillable templates for what statements are about, e.g. rate of a process of a population of a taxonWARP node templates, compiled to OWL
Predicate groupsControlled relationship words plus the qualifiers that say what kind of relation is assertedCausal: CAMO. Interaction: Relation Ontology (RO)
Overall shapeStatement, proposition, evidence line, agent, contributionSEPIO
ScopeOne case, some cases, typical cases, all casesWARP, after Vogt's semantic unit types
Evidential basisStudy design, evidence object, practitioner and Indigenous knowledge as evidenceWARP, using CAMO's evidence types

The warp (fixed threads) is the SEPIO frame, scope and evidence. The weft (woven across, changing) is the node templates and predicate groups. Adding a new kind of statement means adding weft, never changing the warp.

Everything is exchanged as a StatementSet: one file holding agents, nodes, node definitions, statements and propositions for each predicate group, synthesis assessments, study results and documents. Objects refer to each other by id.

SEPIO distinguishes two modes of statement, and WARP uses both:

  • Assertion Mode: the statement puts a proposition forward as true. Every annotated statement (CausalStatement, InteractionStatement) is in this mode, and leaves SEPIO's direction, strength and score empty.
  • Proposition Assessment Mode: the statement says how well the evidence supports a proposition. SynthesisAssessment is in this mode, and must fill direction and strength.

Nodes and node templates

A node is what a statement is about: one end of the subject → predicate → object link. Nodes are recorded as CAMO CausalNodes (despite the name, interaction statements use them too), with a name, an entity type and an entity term.

A node is a variable, not a change

Nodes carry no direction of change. "Increased", "decreased" and "removed" are not part of the node; the predicate carries the sign. So "planting bloodroot prevents garlic mustard population growth rate" rather than "...causes decreased garlic mustard". That lets statements finding increases and decreases meet on the same node.

Node templates

A node template is a fillable shape. Filling one gives the node a precise, machine-readable meaning (an OWL class), a generated label and a definition, all built from ontology terms. Templates are about shape, not topic: population_of_taxon serves deer, garlic mustard and bees alike.

TemplateShapeBlanks filled fromExample
population_of_taxon⟨taxon⟩ populationtaxon: NCBITaxonOdocoileus virginianus population

intervention_involving_taxon⟨action⟩ of ⟨taxon⟩action: ELMO; taxon: NCBITaxonplanting of Sanguinaria canadensis

Each template also has a logical definition. In words, the garlic mustard node is:

a rate that is a characteristic of some (population growth that has participant some (population of organisms that has member some Alliaria petiolata))

Because these are OWL definitions, a reasoner can tell that two nodes built from the same template and terms are the same thing, and that a node about a species sits under a node about its genus.

A node can be recorded without a template filling; it just won't have a logical definition yet. CAMO nodes record taxa as Wikidata QIDs in entity_term, while template fillings use NCBITaxon IDs. Both are kept, and are to be crosswalked with SSSOM.

Predicate groups

A predicate group is one kind of statement. Each brings its own predicate list, its own predicate qualifiers, a statement class and a proposition class. Everything else (scope, hedging conventions, CARE, evidence lines, assessments, proposition derivation) is shared. Two groups exist today.

Interaction statements relate population nodes and qualify the predicate with effect_on_subject and effect_on_object (positive, negative, neutral, not_reported): −/+ for predation, +/+ for mutualism, and so on.

Planned groups (occurrence, composition, TEK statements via IUS) will slot in the same way.

Anatomy of a statement

Every annotated statement, in any group, shares these fields (from EcoStatement and SEPIO Statement):

FieldHolds
idIdentifier, e.g. warp:statement/murphy2005-c1.
subject, predicate, objectNode id, predicate from the group's list, node id. Required.
negatedTrue if the statement says the relationship does not hold. Defaults to false.
scopeSemantic unit type (below). Recommended; needed for grouping.
statementTextA normalized rendering of the statement.
original_textThe statement as worded in its source: verbatim sentence, notebook line, transcript passage.
source_spansText spans (with character offsets and document section) grounding the statement.

dateAuthoredWhen the statement was made (by its author, not the annotator).
contributionsWho made the statement: at least one contribution with role author.
recordMetadataWho recorded it in EcoWeaver, and when (role annotator, dateRecordCreated).
careConsent, access and knowledge-holder metadata (below).
hasEvidenceLinesThe statement's own evidence lines (below).
propositionDerived. Filled by the derivation script, never by annotators.

Extra fields on causal statements

FieldHolds
causal_languageStatement-level hedging, from CAMO (adapted from Yu et al. 2019): direct_causal, uncertain_causal, associational, no_relationship. Captures the text's commitment, not the strength of the evidence.
predicate_qualifiersWhat kind of causal relation is asserted (below).
comparatorWhat the effect was assessed relative to, one entry per comparison arm: untreated_control, reference_undisturbed, pre_treatment_baseline, alternative_treatment, passive_management_arm, spatial_or_temporal_control, not_reported, not_applicable.
contextConditions under which the statement holds: scope conditions, geographic, temporal and ecosystem scope.
ecosystem_contextAn IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology functional group, grounded in ELMO.
temporal_extentThe timescale of the effect itself: duration, lag, observation grain and seasons.
camo_edge_idThe CAMO edge this statement was migrated from, if any.
Negation and hedging must agree On a causal statement, negated is true exactly when causal_language is no_relationship. The checks reject a statement where the two disagree.

Hedging belongs to the statement (it's how cautiously this author spoke). Scope belongs to the proposition (it's part of what is said), and is copied onto the statement so propositions can be derived.

Causal features (predicate qualifiers)

An RDF predicate is a single term, so finer distinctions about the causal relation go in predicate_qualifiers, following the Biolink pattern. These are CAMO's philosophical-account and causal-feature layers, grouped together. Most take a feature assertion value: explicitly_asserted, implicitly_assumed, explicitly_denied or not_addressed.

QualifierQuestionValues
philosophical_accountsWhich account of causation does the claim invoke? (Illari & Russo)counterfactual, probabilistic, interventionist, transmission, mechanistic, regularity, inus_component, agency (several allowed)
necessity, sufficiencyIs C claimed necessary / sufficient for E?feature assertion
causal_directionIs the direction C → E asserted, and on what grounds?status: asserted, uncertain, bidirectional, not_addressed; evidence: experimental, temporal_precedence, theoretical, natural_experiment, structural_model
temporal_orderingIs C claimed to precede E?feature assertion
mediationIs a mediating pathway identified?status, mediator node ids, pathway description
moderationDoes something modify the effect?status, moderator node ids, interaction type
effect_strengthHow big is the effect?status, reported value ("60% reduction"), numeric value, qualitative (strong…negligible), dose-response
specificity, stability, proportionalitySpecific cause? Stable across contexts? Proportional?feature assertion
determinismIs the process framed as deterministic?deterministic_process, indeterministic_process, epistemic_probability_only, ambiguous, not_addressed
proximate_distalImmediate trigger or root cause?proximate, distal, both_specified, not_addressed
contributing_soleSole cause or one of several?sole_cause, contributing_cause, not_addressed
reversibilityDoes removing C undo E?reversible, irreversible, partially_reversible, hysteresis, not_addressed

Qualifiers are not part of a proposition's identity. If two papers disagree about reversibility, that shows up as variation across statements of one proposition, not as two propositions.

Two kinds of "direction" and "strength"

SEPIO and CAMO both use the words direction and strength, for different things. WARP keeps them apart by name:

Evidential (SEPIO)Causal (CAMO)
Directiondirection / directionOfEvidenceProvided: does this support, dispute, or stay neutral toward a proposition?predicate_qualifiers.causal_direction: does C act on E, E on C, or both?
Strengthstrength / strengthOfEvidenceProvided: how strongly does the evidence bear on the proposition?predicate_qualifiers.effect_strength: how large is the effect?
WhereEvidence lines and synthesis assessments onlyAnnotated causal statements

A large effect measured in a weak study is effect_strength: strong with strengthOfEvidenceProvided: weak.

Scope

Scope says what a statement is about, following Vogt's semantic unit types for statement units. (The definitions below are working glosses still to be checked against Vogt's own.)

ValueAboutRendered prefix
assertionalParticular things: this plot, this population, this burn. Field observations and case reports are usually assertional."In a particular case, …"
contingentSome, but not necessarily all, instances of a type: something that can or sometimes does happen."Sometimes, …"
prototypicalWhat typically holds for instances of a type, allowing exceptions. The usual scope of an ecological generalization."Typically, …"
universalAll instances of a type, without exception."Always, …"
unclearCannot be determined from the source. Blocks grouping until resolved.(not grouped)

Scope replaces CAMO's token_or_type. When migrating, token → assertional and type → prototypical by default, and each is flagged for a human to check: most ecological generalizations hold typically, not universally.

Propositions and identity

Nobody writes propositions by hand (except open questions). A script groups statements under identity rule warp-prop-identity/3:

Two statements share a proposition when group, subject node, predicate, object node, negated and scope all match exactly.

Everything else stays on the statement and is aggregated, not used for identity: hedging, causal features, comparator, context, evidence.

Same or different?

Statement AStatement BSame proposition?
Planting bloodroot prevents GM growth rate, typical (paper 1, "reduces")Same, typical (paper 2, "may reduce")Yes. Hedging differs, but it's not identity.
…typical…one plot (assertional)No. Scope differs.
…prevents……does not prevent… (negated)No. They are opposite propositions; a synthesis weighs both.
…in temperate deciduous forest…in oldfieldsYes, under rule 3. Context isn't identity (yet; see open decisions).
Causal: deer prevents XInteraction: deer eats XNo. Different group and predicate.

Open questions

A proposition written by people with status: open_question represents a question nobody has answered: an empty cell in an evidence gap map, or a practitioner's question in TREK. It keeps its chosen id. When a statement matches, it becomes asserted, its text is replaced by the rendered fact, and the original question is kept in description. If those statements go away, it reverts to open_question. It's never deleted by re-derivation.

When the rule changes

The identity rule is a modelling decision. Changing it regroups statements, so every proposition records the rule version that made it, and derived proposition ids change with each version (open-question ids don't). Version 1 had no group; version 2 added it; version 3 changed the non-negated key word from "asserted" to "affirmed" to avoid clashing with the proposition status.

Evidence lines

An evidence line (EcoEvidenceLine) is one argument for or against a proposition, built from evidence items of one kind. A statement with an experiment and a supporting model has two evidence lines.

FieldHolds
evidence_typeKind of study or reasoning. Required. (Replaces SEPIO's generic subtype, which stays empty.)
evidence_objectDoes the evidence bear on correlation (difference-making), mechanism (production), or both? (The Russo–Williamson distinction.)
directionOfEvidenceProvidedsupports, disputes or neutral. Required.
strengthOfEvidenceProvidedstrong, moderate, weak or not_assessed.
evidence_item_refsIds of the evidence: study results, documents, or other statements. By reference, not copied in.
targetPropositionThe proposition argued about. On a statement's own lines this is derived (it's the statement's proposition).
contributionsWho judged direction and strength (role evidence_assessor). If absent, the judgement is attributed to the statement's annotator, or the assessment's author.

Study results

For evidence from data, the recommended level of detail is an EcoStudyResult: a summary of one study arm or plot group about one node (mean, variability, n), traceable to its source dataset. Evidence lines point at these by id.

Synthesis assessments

A SynthesisAssessment is a statement too, made by EcoWeaver's synthesis pipeline or a review team, in Proposition Assessment Mode. Unlike annotated statements, it targets a chosen proposition, and must give:

  • direction: supports, disputes or neutral;
  • strength: a GRADE-style certainty grade, which controls how the finding is worded downstream;
  • optionally evidence_balance: supportive, mixed, conflicting, insufficient, no_evidence, not_assessed.
Certainty gradeRendered as
highX increases Y
moderateX probably increases Y
lowX may increase Y
very_lowX might increase Y
not_assessed(not formally assessed)

Its evidence lines cite other people's statements as evidence items.

How a case report supports a generalization

In the worked example, the assessment targets the prototypical proposition "typically, planting bloodroot prevents garlic mustard population growth". It has two evidence lines: the randomized experiment (moderate, citing the published statement) and practitioner experience (weak, citing the notebook statement). The notebook statement has its own assertional proposition, but enters here as evidence for the general one. Overall: supports, low certainty, supportive balance.

Agents, roles and CARE

Agents

Every person, organization, community or piece of software is an EcoAgent with an id (an ORCID or ROR id where one exists and the agent agrees, otherwise a local warp:agent/… id, which may be a pseudonym) and an agent_kind:

researcher · practitioner · indigenous_knowledge_holder · community_member · organization · synthesis_pipeline

Contributions and roles

Credit is given through contributions, each naming an agent and a role. Each role has one proper place, and the checks enforce it:

RoleMeaningGoes on
authorMade the statement or assessmentthe statement's contributions
evidence_assessorJudged an evidence line's direction and strengththe evidence line's contributions
annotatorRecorded it in EcoWeaver: provenance of the record, not the knowledgethe statement's recordMetadata.contributions
reviewerChecked someone else's annotation or assessmentany

CARE metadata

Practitioner and Indigenous knowledge appear in two roles: as statements in their own right, with the knowledge holder as author, and as evidence for other statements. Recording them only as evidence would make knowledge holders sources for other people's claims, never people making their own.

A statement authored by an indigenous_knowledge_holder or community_member must carry care metadata, or the checks refuse it.

CARE fieldHolds
knowledge_holdersAgent ids of those recognised as holding the knowledge. Often the author, but not always: knowledge relayed by a community member may be held by an elder or the community.
communityCommunity or nation with authority over the knowledge.
consent_referencePointer to the consent or agreement it was recorded under.
access_levelRequired: open, attribution_required, community_restricted, restricted.
local_contexts_labelsLocal Contexts TK/BC label or notice URIs.
protocolsCommunity protocols governing use, in the community's words.

Writing a precise suggestion

The contributor template is always fine. If you'd rather be exact, paste a statement in WARP's own format into the issue. Leave out proposition and targetProposition (they're derived), and don't invent node ids: describe new nodes in words, or propose a template filling.

causal_statements:
  - id: warp:statement/<short-id>
    type: CausalStatement
    subject: node:planting_of_sanguinaria_canadensis       # existing node id, or describe it
    predicate: prevents
    object: node:rate_of_population_growth_of_alliaria_petiolata
    negated: false                                         # true iff causal_language is no_relationship
    scope: prototypical
    causal_language: direct_causal
    statementText: Planting bloodroot reduces the population growth rate of garlic mustard.
    original_text: "<verbatim sentence>"
    reportedIn: [<DOI or citation>]
    contributions:
      - {role: author, contributor: <who made the statement>}
    comparator:
      - {status: explicitly_asserted, comparator_type: untreated_control}
    predicate_qualifiers:
      philosophical_accounts: [interventionist]
      causal_direction: {status: asserted, evidence_for_direction: experimental}
    hasEvidenceLines:
      - evidence_type: randomized_experiment
        evidence_object: correlation
        directionOfEvidenceProvided: supports
        strengthOfEvidenceProvided: not_assessed

To propose a new node with a template filling:

node_definitions:
  - node: node:<proposed_id>
    template: quality_of_process_of_population
    fillers:
      - {var: quality, value: "PATO:0000161", label: rate}
      - {var: process, value: "PCO:0000011", label: population growth}
      - {var: taxon,   value: "NCBITaxon:126270", label: Alliaria petiolata}

Look terms up in OLS. Each template restricts which ontologies may fill each blank (see the template table), e.g. taxa must be NCBITaxon, not Wikidata.

Coming from CAMO

Most of a CAMO CausalEdge becomes the predicate layer of a CausalStatement. Hedging and scope belong to the speaker, and evidence type belongs to the evidence. Annotators moving over should read WARP for CAMO annotators, which covers the changed habits and a full worked translation.

Open decisions you can weigh in on

WARP is a draft. These are live questions; issues with views or real examples are welcome.

  • Identity. Should any context (ecosystem type, comparator) be part of a proposition's identity? It would make propositions narrower and evidence gap maps sparser.
  • Scope for annotators. Is the four-way scope choice teachable, or should annotation tools ask "about one place and time, or in general?" and map the answer?
  • Where certainty lives. Should a paper-level certainty grade go on its evidence lines' strength, or only on synthesis assessments?
  • Manual vs automatic. Recording whether a statement was curated by a person or extracted by a pipeline (ECO manual/automatic assertion), and what a human-reviewed extraction counts as.
  • Study quality. Choosing an appraisal scheme, recorded separately from evidence strength.
  • Agent identity. When practitioners and knowledge holders get an ORCID versus a pseudonymous id, and who decides.
  • Template ontology. Whether a rate is a PATO quality of a process or (per BFO 2020) a process profile.
  • Evidence counting. Whether syntheses count studies, statements or study arms, and how evidence types are weighted.
